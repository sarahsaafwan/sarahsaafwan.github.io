INDEPENDENT WRITING / EDITORIAL WORK

Writing things I actually want to say.

My Substack is the part of my work where I don’t have to begin with a product, a brand brief, or a format chosen for an algorithm. I can begin with a question, an observation, or a feeling that doesn’t seem quite settled, and give it the space to develop. I write about personal experiences, emotions, growth, and the way ordinary things can take on more meaning than we expect them to.

What matters to me is finding the right perspective, not simply choosing a topic that sounds reflective. A piece needs a reason to exist. Sometimes that means taking apart a familiar idea and asking whether I really agree with it. Sometimes it means writing about a feeling that I can recognize but haven’t yet worked out how to explain. In either case, I want the writing to reach an actual thought rather than circle around a polished-sounding sentence.

How I work on a piece

I often begin with fragments: a line, a question, a disagreement, a memory, or something I noticed. From there, the work is to find out what connects them. I develop the structure as the argument becomes clearer, then revise sentences that feel too vague, overly dramatic, or unlike something I’d actually say. Being personal isn’t the same thing as being imprecise. The more specific the observation, the more likely it is to resonate with somebody else.

Editing is a significant part of the process. I look for places where a paragraph repeats an earlier point, where an example doesn’t really support the idea, or where a neat conclusion is trying to finish a thought that isn’t finished yet. Sometimes the best choice is to leave a little uncertainty in the piece rather than force an answer.

Writing as part of a wider creative practice

Independent writing is not the same work as marketing copy, and I don’t want to present it as if the two are interchangeable. But writing regularly has made me more sensitive to tone, audience, and whether words communicate something specific. I carry that attention into website writing, concept development, and editing, even when the format and objective are entirely different.
