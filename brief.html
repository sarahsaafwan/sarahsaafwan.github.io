Before the first conversation

A brief worth discussing.

The downloadable worksheet is designed to reduce confusion before a first conversation. It records the information most likely to affect scope: objectives, audience, deliverables, languages, references, timelines and approvals. It is a planning aid, not a contract or a final price.

Download fillable brief ↓Download text version ↓

What to include.

  • Your goals and audience
  • Services, deliverables, and formats
  • References and available assets
  • Website scope and language needs
  • Budget, timeline, and handover

Fill in what you know. Leave anything undecided blank; we can work through it together.

Save your completed PDF, then attach it to your enquiry email. The text version can be completed in any text editor or copied into the enquiry form.

The worksheet does not set a final price or create a project agreement.
