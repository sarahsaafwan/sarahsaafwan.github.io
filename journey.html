From first conversation to handover.

I prefer to clarify expectations before production begins: what is being made, who supplies the source material, which formats are needed and who approves the work. The project agreement specifies the price, timing, revisions, permissions and handover. The steps below explain a possible workflow, not a fixed policy for every enquiry.
