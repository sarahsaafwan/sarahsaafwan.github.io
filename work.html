SELECTED WORK

Work, with the story behind it.

I wanted this section to show more than finished visuals. A finished piece tells you what somebody produced, but not necessarily how they approached it, what they had to consider, or the choices that made one format more appropriate than another. That’s why the case studies go beyond thumbnails and service labels.

The projects here span brand collaborations, marketing research, campaign-related creative production, and independent publishing. Some were developed as part of a professional role; others grew out of ideas I pursued independently. I describe the work in the context in which it happened, including my actual role, the format, and the particular decisions I worked through.

You can browse by discipline, or simply open the projects that interest you. The most revealing details are usually inside.
