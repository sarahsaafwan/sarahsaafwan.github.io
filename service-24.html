UGC for brand-owned channels

On-camera and faceless videos, demonstrations, unboxing, tutorials, lifestyle, voiceover and genuine experience-based reviews are scoped for brands. Recording is in English or Lebanese Arabic. Personal social posting and creator-account advertising authorization are not offered.

Beauty, skincare, fashion, accessories, lifestyle, technology/apps, wellness and food/beverage categories are individually reviewed. Claims must be truthful and based on genuine experience.

A gifted product does not automatically create an obligation or grant advertising, raw footage, unlimited edits or perpetual rights. Deliverables, shipping/customs, deadline, disclosure and usage are accepted separately.

Exclusivity and raw footage require a custom quote. AI training, synthetic voice/likeness, misleading re-edits and transfer are excluded unless an explicit grant is separately agreed. No automatic buyout or renewal.
