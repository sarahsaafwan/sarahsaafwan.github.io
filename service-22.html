Services / Scope by agreement

Additional Revision Work

Additional revision work applies when an agreed project needs work beyond its included scope. The original brief, the requested change, and the service involved determine what needs to be done. Corrections to agreed specifications are treated separately from paid additions; the approved package terms below retain that distinction.
