Tell me what you’re working on.

I’m open to discussing freelance projects, creative collaborations, and professional marketing opportunities. If you have a brief, feel free to send it. If the project is still at the idea stage, that’s fine too; a little context about what you want to achieve is enough to start a conversation.

For creative work, it helps to know what the project is about, which deliverables you have in mind, whether you already have materials to work from, and the timeframe you’re considering. For a role, send the position and a sense of the responsibilities involved. I’ll respond based on the scope and my availability.
