SERVICES

Creative work, with a clear purpose.

Not every project needs the same kind of support. Some brands have their direction figured out and need someone to produce the content. Others have plenty of ideas but need help deciding which ones are worth pursuing. Sometimes the problem is the way something is written, edited, organized, or presented. My services cover those different stages, from research and concept development through to design, writing, video editing, presentations, and websites.

I prefer to begin with the job the work needs to do. A carousel intended to explain a product needs a different structure from one made to entertain. A video assembled from existing footage needs clear editorial decisions even if nothing new is filmed. A website has to make information easy to find and understand, not simply look attractive in a screenshot. The package catalogue provides structured starting points, and individual or custom scopes can be discussed when the work calls for something more specific.

Social media content design

Static posts, stories, and multi-slide designs planned for how people actually encounter content on a phone. I consider the point of the post, which information needs prominence, and how the design carries the reader through it. The aim is to make a finished piece that belongs to the brand while remaining clear at social-media size.

Carousel and post production

A useful carousel has to give someone a reason to swipe and a reason to stay. I work with structure, copy placement, visual consistency, hierarchy, and the relationship between consecutive slides rather than treating them as a set of unrelated graphics.

Short-form video editing

Editing provided footage and assets into purposeful short-form pieces, with attention to the first seconds, selection and sequence of shots, pacing, sound, transitions, and the way a video needs to work on its intended platform.

Content planning

Developing content directions and calendars that account for what a brand needs to say, what it can realistically produce, and how different post types contribute to a coherent presence. Planning should make production easier, not create an impressive-looking document nobody can use.

Creative concept development

Finding the idea behind the execution: researching references, developing angles, working through how a concept could appear across formats, and making the direction clear enough for actual production.

Marketing research

Competitor, content, and advertising research intended to support choices. The focus is on identifiable patterns and useful observations, with a distinction between what the examples show and what might be inferred from them.

Writing and copy

Website copy, social copy, and editorial material that takes audience, tone, structure, and intended use seriously. The right wording depends on context; the goal is clarity and character rather than an endlessly repeated brand voice template.

Brand visuals and presentations

Visual directions, style references, layouts, and presentations organized to help an idea communicate clearly. These services can support a defined creative need without pretending every assignment requires a complete identity overhaul.

Websites and updates

Designing and organizing responsive website experiences that make it easy to understand what someone offers, browse their work, and get in touch. Scope can range from a focused landing page to a larger content-driven site, with maintenance and additional pages considered separately.

UGC production

Creating on-camera or faceless brand content delivered directly for use on a client’s own channels. Production, revisions, usage licensing, advertising rights, and additional footage are scoped separately as applicable.

How a project moves forward

You tell me what you’re working on and what you need from me. I review the scope, materials, timeline, and intended use, then we agree on the deliverables and terms. From there, production follows the brief, feedback is consolidated at the agreed review points, and the final files are delivered in the formats needed. Nothing about the process needs to be mysterious, but it should be specific enough that both sides know what to expect.
