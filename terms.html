Services / Scope by agreement

Project policies.

These service policies guide quotations. Project-specific scope and legal terms are defined in your written agreement. Cancellation and refund provisions remain draft pending review.

Payments

Small projects use 100% upfront; medium creative and paid UGC projects use 50/50; websites and larger projects use 40/30/30. The international small-project threshold is under $100. Egyptian and Lebanese small-project thresholds remain project-specific until set. Project classification, milestones and dates are confirmed in the quotation.

Regional payment arrangements

International: USD via PayPal where supported. Egypt: EGP via InstaPay or eligible PayPal payments. Lebanon: USD only, with OMT or Whish arrangements subject to confirmation. Transfer eligibility, processing fees and final payment arrangements are confirmed in the quotation.

Revisions and extras

Each package includes its stated revision allowance. Ordinary included revisions refine the agreed scope. Additional work is charged according to its service category. Minor design/edit changes, copy changes and creative development use their respective hourly rates where published; additional pages use the relevant page package. Reshoots, new concepts, languages and deliverables are scoped under the relevant service. A custom quotation is required where no rate is available. Corrections needed to meet agreed specifications are not paid additional revisions.

Feedback and project pauses

Client feedback deadlines and scheduling-pause thresholds are defined per project. The written agreement will address delays, pauses, restart availability and any separately agreed costs. No automatic pause deadline or restart fee is established here.

Correction windows

The correction window is seven calendar days for creative deliverables and fourteen calendar days for websites, subject to applicable legal rights. Corrections address agreed specifications; new features and expanded scope are separate.

Licensing and permitted use

UGC advertising is offered only through brand-owned accounts. Creator-account advertising and posting on personal social media are not offered. Advertising license fees are 25% for 30 days, 50% for 90 days, 80% for six months and 120% for twelve months, shown only as indicative licensing estimates subject to confirmation. Organic-use duration is negotiated per project. Existing organic posts may remain visible after expiration only when explicitly agreed. Raw footage, exclusivity, perpetual usage and copyright buyouts require custom quotations; selecting a duration does not grant rights.

Ownership, files and handover

Final exports are included. Handover follows the selected package. Editable design files and video source files are included only where the package specifies them. UGC raw footage and original logo vector masters are not automatically included. Ownership, permitted use, third-party licenses and any transfer are defined in the written agreement; payment alone does not create a copyright buyout. Client work may appear in the portfolio only with written permission, including permission already granted in the project agreement.

Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation and refund provisions remain draft and require review. The agreement will distinguish completed work, work not provided and authorized external costs. No blanket non-refundable rule, cancellation penalty or automatic refund formula is established here.

E-commerce review

Custom quotation required. Platform, products, variants, inventory, payment providers, shipping, taxes, integrations, checkout testing and client handover are reviewed before scope is accepted. Capabilities and payment integrations are not guaranteed until tested for the specific platform and client. The estimator does not collect payments, enable checkout or book projects.
