Project Policies

Working Together

Every project is different, but the process shouldn’t be confusing. Whether we’re working on a single creative deliverable or building something larger, I want us to have a clear understanding of what’s included, how the work will move forward, and what to expect along the way.

The policies below explain how I generally approach payments, revisions, timelines, usage rights, and project handovers. Your individual quotation and written agreement will outline the arrangements specific to your project.

01

Booking & Payments

+

Getting started

Before we begin, you’ll receive a quotation outlining the agreed deliverables, project scope, pricing, payment schedule, and relevant timelines. Work is booked once we’ve agreed on the terms and the required initial payment has been received.

How payments are structured

Payment schedules depend on the size and nature of the project:

Payment schedules by project type
Project typePayment schedule
Smaller projects100% upfront
Mid-sized creative projects and paid UGC50% to begin, 50% at the agreed milestone
Websites and larger projects40% at the start, 30% at an agreed development milestone, 30% at the final milestone

For international projects, the smaller-project threshold is generally below $100. Thresholds for projects in Egypt and Lebanon are determined individually.

Project classification, milestones, and exact payment dates are specified in the quotation.

Payment methods

I work with clients internationally, in Egypt, and in Lebanon.

  • International: USD through PayPal, where supported.
  • Egypt: EGP through InstaPay or eligible PayPal arrangements.
  • Lebanon: USD through OMT or Whish, where available and agreed.

Applicable transfer fees, processing costs, and payment arrangements are discussed before payment.

02

Revisions & Additional Work

+

Making sure the work is right

Creative work often involves adjustments, which is why each package specifies an included revision allowance. These revisions are intended to refine what we’ve already agreed to create, whether that’s adjusting a layout, revising wording, or improving an edit.

The number of included revision rounds depends on the package selected.

A revision is different from changing the original brief. Refining the text on an agreed carousel is a revision; requesting an entirely different concept is a change in scope.

What happens when you need something extra?

Additional deliverables, new creative concepts, substantial changes, or work beyond the agreed scope are discussed and priced before proceeding.

Depending on the request, additional work may be charged using a published hourly rate, an existing service package, or a custom quotation.

This can include additional design or video editing, copywriting, website pages, alternative concepts, new language versions, and reshoots.

Corrections necessary to meet the originally agreed specifications are not treated as paid additional revisions.

03

Timelines, Feedback & Project Pauses

+

Keeping the project moving

Project timelines are established based on scope, complexity, and the materials required to complete the work.

Agreed milestones and delivery dates are included in the project documents.

Some projects depend on receiving feedback, approvals, product samples, brand materials, or other information from the client. Timely communication helps maintain the agreed schedule.

If your project needs to pause

If a project needs to be placed on hold, we’ll review the completed work, outstanding requirements, and realistic availability for resuming.

Feedback deadlines, pause arrangements, restart availability, and any applicable costs are addressed in the project agreement.

There is no universal restart fee or automatic cancellation triggered by a fixed period of inactivity.

04

Corrections After Delivery

+

A little room for the finishing touches

Once the agreed work has been delivered, there’s a defined period to flag anything that doesn’t match the approved specifications.

Standard correction windows:

  • Creative deliverables: 7 calendar days.
  • Website projects: 14 calendar days.

These windows concern corrections within the agreed scope and do not replace applicable legal rights.

For example, an error against the approved design brief is a correction. Adding a completely new website feature after completion is additional work.

05

Content Usage & Licensing

+

Where and how your content can be used

Some projects involve producing content for a brand’s organic channels. Others, particularly UGC, involve separate paid advertising rights.

The quotation specifies what usage is included.

UGC production

I offer on-camera and faceless UGC produced for brands and clients.

The content is delivered for agreed use on the brand’s own channels.

I do not offer publishing UGC on my personal social media accounts or authorizing advertising through my personal creator accounts.

Paid advertising usage

Paid advertising rights are priced separately from content production.

The following are indicative licensing additions calculated as percentages of the agreed production fee:

Indicative advertising licensing additions
Advertising periodAdditional licensing estimate
30 days+25%
90 days+50%
6 months+80%
12 months+120%

These percentages are indicative estimates, not automatic grants of usage rights. Final licensing terms must be agreed in writing.

Organic usage duration, channels, territories, and any agreed permission for previously published organic posts to remain visible after license expiration are determined per project.

Additional rights and materials

Raw footage, exclusivity, perpetual usage, and full copyright buyouts require separate quotations.

Purchasing content does not automatically transfer all associated rights. The written agreement specifies permitted use and any rights being transferred.

06

Ownership, Files & Handover

+

What you’ll receive

Every package includes the final agreed deliverables in the export formats specified for the project.

These may include completed images, edited videos, website files, written content, or other agreed assets.

Editable source files are not automatically included with every package.

Design working files, video project files, UGC raw footage, and original logo vector masters are supplied when specifically included in the selected package or written agreement.

File formats, handover arrangements, ownership, permitted usage, third-party licenses, and any transfer of rights are defined in the agreement.

Featuring completed work

I may request permission to feature selected completed projects in my professional portfolio.

Any such use follows the permissions agreed with the client, including permissions already established in the project agreement.

07

Cancellations & Refunds

+

If plans change

Sometimes a project needs to be cancelled or cannot proceed as originally planned.

Financial arrangements should reflect work already completed, services or deliverables not yet provided, and authorized external expenses.

The exact cancellation and refund terms are established in the written agreement.

There is no blanket rule making all payments non-refundable, no universal cancellation penalty, and no automatic refund calculation.

These provisions are being finalized and may be updated following review.

08

Website & E-Commerce Projects

+

A closer look before we begin

Website projects can differ substantially in complexity.

A portfolio or informational website is different from an e-commerce store that handles products, payments, inventory, customer orders, and integrations.

For e-commerce work, technical requirements must be reviewed before a final quotation is provided.

The review may include:

  • Website platform
  • Product catalogue and variants
  • Inventory requirements
  • Shipping arrangements
  • Taxes
  • Payment providers
  • Third-party integrations
  • Checkout testing
  • Handover requirements

Availability of specific features and payment integrations depends on the platform, country, and technical requirements.

Capabilities must be evaluated before being included in the agreed scope.

Custom e-commerce projects are quoted individually.
