Choosing your scope

Understand the difference between services.

The services overlap in subject matter but not necessarily in scope. Content planning defines topics and schedules; creative concepts develop a direction; production turns provided material into finished assets; research provides competitive context. Final deliverables, revision rounds and handover are agreed for each engagement rather than fixed by this comparison.

Service scope comparison
ServiceUseful whenPossible outputBring to the brief
Content designYou need consistent social visualsPosts, stories, promotional graphicsCopy, brand references, sizes
Carousel productionA message needs a clear sequenceA finished carousel or postDraft message and reference assets
Video editingYou have footage ready to shapeShort-form editsFootage, duration, references
Content planningYou need editorial structurePillars and a calendarGoals, channels, audience
Creative conceptsYou need a campaign directionConcepts and moodboardsBrief and production constraints
Marketing researchYou need competitive contextCompetitor advertising analysisMarket and research questions
Website buildingYou need a home for your business or workAgreed pages and handoverPage list, content, features

Website work is a developing offer. Prices, timing, revisions, editable files, and third-party costs are confirmed separately.
