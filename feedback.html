I really enjoy The Sae Talk Podcast because it simply helps me feel seen. The thing that made me ecstatic about it is how every experience mentioned in each episode sounds so familiar, and it really helps me mentally by making me feel like, "Don't worry, we're all on the same page." The way each topic feels so relatable really caught me off guard and made me think, "I should listen to this. I can relate!" I would recommend everyone to check out the podcast because it's so helpful in changing your mentality and perspective, as well as helping you achieve a better lifestyle. There's also a quote from the gratitude episode that has stayed with me and become my favorite: "Gratitude isn't about appreciating everything around us. It's simply a verb, it's about acting grateful."

Mo S.

★★★★★
The Sae Talk
I came across Sarah’s work through her podcast and eventually found myself exploring her website and writing as well. What stood out to me most was how intentional everything feels. There’s a sense of thoughtfulness behind every piece of content that makes it feel personal rather than performative. I especially enjoyed the way complex ideas are broken down into reflections that are easy to relate to. Instead of simply sharing advice, Sarah invites you to think differently about your own experiences, which is something I found refreshing. One idea that stayed with me was that growth doesn’t always come from having all the answers, it often begins with asking better questions. That perspective has genuinely stuck with me. I would absolutely recommend her work to anyone who enjoys thoughtful conversations, meaningful writing, and content that prioritizes depth over noise. It’s the kind of work you don’t just consume, you sit with it afterward.
