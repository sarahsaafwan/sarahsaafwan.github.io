Illustrative editing exercise

What changes when copy becomes clearer.

These fictional editing exercises focus on decisions rather than before-and-after performance claims. A message becomes easier to use when it identifies the action, explains what matters and removes competing instructions. Likewise, separating an overloaded post into a sequence can give each piece of information a purpose.

Personal concepts / Illustrative only

From vague copy to a clear invitation

Before

We have lots of great books and things for everyone. Come and see what we offer.

After

Find your next chapter. Browse thoughtful picks and make time for a quiet read.

Editing choices: a specific action, a recognisable experience, and a shorter message.

From a crowded post to a readable sequence

Before

One post combines the event introduction, agenda, visiting directions, and booking instructions.

After

  1. Introduce the event and its purpose.
  2. Explain what to expect.
  3. Give the place and time.
  4. Finish with one booking action.

Structure choices: one job per slide and a clear next step.
