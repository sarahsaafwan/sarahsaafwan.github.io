Practical answers

Practical answers before we begin.

Find practical answers about timing, revisions, payment, files, and language versions. The details for your project are set out in your agreement.

8 answers

How long does a project take?

Turnaround depends on scope, asset readiness, and availability. A delivery schedule is agreed before work begins.

How many revisions are included?

The number of revision rounds will be stated in your proposal. Additional rounds and changes outside the agreed brief are scoped separately.

What are the payment terms?

Payment schedule, currency, deposit, and final-payment terms will be confirmed in the project agreement. Standard terms are still being finalized.

What should I provide?

Your brief, audience, objectives, brand references, available assets, required formats, and deadline. For websites, include the page list, content, domain details, and desired functionality.

Which files will I receive?

Delivery formats are agreed by service and platform. Editable source files and ownership or usage terms should be specified in the proposal.

Do you offer English and Arabic work?

I write in English and Arabic. Bilingual scope, translation, and the review process are agreed for each project.

What is included in a website project?

Page count, platform, copywriting, language versions, revisions, and handover are agreed in the brief. Hosting, domains, subscriptions, and ongoing maintenance are quoted separately when needed.

Are calculator estimates final quotes?

No. The calculator uses draft rates and planning inputs. Final pricing depends on the agreed scope, availability, and project requirements.
