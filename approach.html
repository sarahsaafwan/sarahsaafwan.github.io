The thinking behind the work

How I approach the work.

I begin by understanding the context: who a piece of work is for, what it needs to communicate and which practical constraints matter. Research and references help me assess possible directions before I move into visual or written execution. During production I review consistency, clarity and the relationship between individual elements; revisions are most useful when they respond to a clear brief rather than changing direction without purpose.
