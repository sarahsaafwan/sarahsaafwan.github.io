Enquiries & feedback

How submitted information is handled.

This notice explains which details the enquiry and feedback forms collect, how they are stored, who can review them, and how publication permissions work.

What is collected

Enquiries include your name, email, enquiry type, service, brief, and any optional budget, deadline, or package enquiry. Feedback includes the fields you submit and your publication permissions. A submission reference, status, and timestamp are stored with each record.

Where it goes

Submitted enquiries and feedback are stored in the website’s Cloudflare R2 storage through Sites hosting. Sarah reviews them in management inboxes protected by the current private site access. Submitting the form does not open an email app or automatically subscribe you to a mailing list.

What may be published

Feedback is unpublished by default. It can appear on the site only after approval and permission to quote it. Name and organisation are displayed only with their separate permissions. Email addresses and project details are excluded from the feedback gallery. Previously published community reflections are attributed on the feedback page.

Browser storage

Market preferences and project-brief drafts use browser storage. Package enquiry links pass the selected service, tier, and market to the contact form. Any enquiry confirmation details saved in this tab can be cleared on the confirmation page. Clearing browser data does not remove submitted records.

Removal and corrections

Contact Sarah with your submission reference to request a correction, withdrawal of feedback permission, or removal. Include enough information to identify your submission; do not send sensitive documents. No fixed retention period or response time has been finalized.

Other services

The website uses Google Fonts. External links, including LinkedIn, use their own privacy practices. Hosting and access services may process technical request data under their own policies.
