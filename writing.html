WRITING & AUDIO

Writing has always been part of the work.

Before I had a clear professional direction, writing was one of the ways I tried to understand what interested me. Sometimes it meant researching a subject and explaining it in an article. Sometimes it meant trying to describe something personal without making it sound bigger or simpler than it was. Later, audio added another layer: learning how to organize thoughts for a listener rather than a reader.

The three projects in this section represent distinct approaches. Thy Academia was educational and editorial, built around planning content for an audience and sustaining an independent publication. The Sae Talk took some of that interest in communication into recorded conversations and episodes. My Substack is a more personal space for long-form pieces in my own voice. They overlap in the importance of language, but they don’t serve the same purpose, and I don’t want to pretend they do.
