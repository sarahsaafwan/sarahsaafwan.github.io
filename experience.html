EXPERIENCE

How the work developed.

My experience combines professional brand work, independent creative projects, and research-based writing. Those categories have given me different responsibilities. A marketing internship means developing ideas and research within a broader team’s work. A brand creators program involves interpreting a product and delivering content against a defined brief. Building an independent platform means making and managing most of the decisions yourself. I value the differences because each taught me something I couldn’t have learned from the others alone.

DAXUEN | Marketing Intern

At DAXUEN, I’ve worked across several parts of fashion marketing rather than one isolated production task. My experience includes campaign concept development, content planning, AI-assisted image and video work, influencer-marketing exposure, competitor research, and advertising analysis. The creative work has required turning broad ideas into directions specific enough to produce and review. The research side has involved examining public advertising from comparable streetwear brands and organizing findings into useful observations. Together, those responsibilities have helped me understand how research, creative execution, and campaign decisions influence one another.

BellaBelles Egypt | Creators Program · 2026

The BellaBelles Creators Program gave me the opportunity to develop a small body of social-first work for a jewelry brand, rather than one repeated type of product post. I worked on concepting, graphics, a personality-led carousel, short-form styling videos, and animation. Each deliverable called for a different treatment: character and audience identification for the carousel, humor for the static design, product detail for the videos, and pacing for the animated wishlist. It was an opportunity to practice adapting a creative idea to the product and platform instead of relying on a single visual formula.

Thy Academia | Founder · 2022–2025

I founded and ran Thy Academia as an educational publishing project centered on student life, studying, and wellbeing. My responsibilities covered subject research, writing, editing, graphic design, editorial planning, social content, and the ongoing management of the platform. Working without a separate team meant learning how every stage connected, from deciding whether a topic was worth covering to preparing the finished piece for publication. Thy Academia was one of my earliest sustained experiences of taking full responsibility for a creative project.

The Sae Talk | Creator and Host · Completed

I created and hosted The Sae Talk as an independent audio project. Developing episodes required finding meaningful subjects, preparing a conversational structure, recording, editing, and preparing the show for release. Podcasting helped me understand storytelling in a format where pacing and tone are heard rather than read. The project has ended, but it remains an important part of my experience with editorial development and digital production.

Independent Content Creation | 2024–2026

Before moving my focus toward behind-the-scenes marketing and client production, I explored creating and editing content independently. That work gave me hands-on experience with the practical realities of short-form media: finding an angle, filming, selecting footage, editing for pace, and adjusting a piece for the platform where it would appear. It also helped me understand what kinds of creative work I wanted to pursue professionally. I now apply those skills primarily to brand and client projects rather than personal creator publishing.

Life Sculptor | Research Volunteer · 2022–2023

My earlier volunteer work involved researching psychology-related subjects and working with information intended to help people understand a topic more clearly. It strengthened habits that became useful in later editorial and marketing work: approaching a question carefully, reading beyond the surface, and considering how information needs to be organized for an audience. It forms part of the research foundation behind my writing and content interests.

Sarah Safwan | Independent Writing

Alongside other projects, I maintain a practice of long-form writing. It involves developing subjects from observations and experiences, working through structure, revising tone, and making sure a piece earns its conclusion. Writing has been a continuing part of my work even as my professional focus has moved more directly toward marketing and creative production.
